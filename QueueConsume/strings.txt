PLUGIN_QUEUECONSUME
	EN	Queue Consume

PLUGIN_QUEUECONSUME_DESC
	EN	Removes each track from the play queue once it has finished playing or has been skipped. Jumping directly to another track in the queue leaves in place the track you jumped away from. Enable Queue Consume for each player in Settings -> Player -> Extra Settings -> Queue Consume.

PLUGIN_QUEUECONSUME_BASIC_DESC
	EN	The options below apply to every player.

PLUGIN_QUEUECONSUME_ENABLE
	EN	Queue consume

PLUGIN_QUEUECONSUME_ENABLE_DESC
	EN	Remove tracks from this player's queue after they have played or been skipped.

PLUGIN_QUEUECONSUME_ON_PREVIOUS
	EN	Consume when skipping backwards

PLUGIN_QUEUECONSUME_ON_PREVIOUS_DESC
	EN	Also remove the current track when Previous is pressed. Off by default, so that Previous stays usable. (Global option, valid for all players.)

PLUGIN_QUEUECONSUME_LAST_TRACK
	EN	Consume the final track

PLUGIN_QUEUECONSUME_LAST_TRACK_DESC
	EN	Remove the last entry of the queue when playback reaches the end, leaving the queue empty. Turn this off to keep the final track. (Global option, valid for all players.)

PLUGIN_CONSUMEMODE
	EN	Consume Mode

PLUGIN_CONSUMEMODE_DESC
	EN	Removes each track from the play queue once it has finished playing or has been skipped, the same way MPD's consume mode works. Jumping directly to another track in the queue leaves the track you jumped away from in place.

PLUGIN_CONSUMEMODE_BASIC_DESC
	EN	These options apply to every player. Consume mode itself is switched on per player, under Settings - Player.

PLUGIN_CONSUMEMODE_ENABLE
	EN	Consume mode

PLUGIN_CONSUMEMODE_ENABLE_DESC
	EN	Remove tracks from this player's queue after they have played or been skipped.

PLUGIN_CONSUMEMODE_ON_PREVIOUS
	EN	Consume when skipping backwards

PLUGIN_CONSUMEMODE_ON_PREVIOUS_DESC
	EN	Also remove the current track when Previous is pressed. Off by default, so that Previous stays usable.

PLUGIN_CONSUMEMODE_LAST_TRACK
	EN	Consume the final track

PLUGIN_CONSUMEMODE_LAST_TRACK_DESC
	EN	Remove the last entry of the queue when playback reaches the end, leaving the queue empty. Turn this off to keep the final track.
